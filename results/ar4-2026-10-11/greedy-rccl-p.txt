Before the transaction occurs, the following conditions must be met:
*   **Topology:** Two GPUs (GPU A and GPU B) are connected to different downstream ports of a PCIe Switch. The Switch is connected to the Root Complex (CPU) via an Upstream Port.
*   **Address Space:** GPU A has a memory region (e.g., a buffer) mapped into the system physical address space. GPU B has been programmed (via DMA descriptors or driver setup) to write to the physical address of GPU A’s buffer.
*   **P2P Support:** The PCIe Switch must support P2P routing. Most modern switches do, but some older or low-cost switches may force all traffic through the Root Complex (CPU) if P2P is disabled or unsupported.
